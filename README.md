# CHRONO-DEX // The 300-Million-Year Continental & Anatomical Atlas

> An interactive, naturalist-grade paleontological and anatomical atlas spanning 300 million years of Pokémon continental drift and speciation across all 1,025 official species.

**Live Application**: [https://chrono-dex.vercel.app](https://chrono-dex.vercel.app)  
**Authors**: `sm000ky × Zero Two`

---

## Highlights

### 1. Dynamic Geological Epoch Art Styles & Ambient Soundscapes
When sliding through the 5 Geological Epochs, the entire web interface, card frames, typography, textures, and continuous procedural Web Audio ambient soundscapes transform in real-time:
* **Epoch I: Primordial Poké-Pangea (300 Mya)** — Archaic cave petroglyphs carved into volcanic basalt, ochre earth tones, deep magma rumble & sub-bass stone friction.
* **Epoch II: Mesozoic Continental Fracture (100 Mya)** — Antique Ernst Haeckel sepia lithograph & botanical parchment, rich walnut ink, clockwork astrolabe ticks.
* **Epoch III: Feudal Antiquity / Hisui Era (3,000 BCE)** — Traditional Japanese Ukiyo-e woodblock print, vermilion lacquer borders, gold-flecked washi, tranquil temple bells & bamboo wind chimes.
* **Epoch IV: Victorian & Modern Oceanic Era (Present)** — Crisp high-contrast botanical museum specimen cards, solid drop-shadow plates, coastal breeze surf & typewriter detents.
* **Epoch V: Paradox Temporal Horizon (Area Zero / Future)** — Cybernetic holographic blueprint, electric cyan & neon amber neon glow, quantum chorus synthesizer drone.

### 2. The 4-Layer Tactile Anatomical Slicer
Inspect any of the 1,025 Pokémon with four scientific diagnostic layers:
* **Layer I: Dermis & Integument** — Binomial Latin/Greek taxonomy, skin/fur/scale morphology.
* **Layer II: Osteology & Skeletal Framework** — Real-time X-Ray radiogram shader, computed Bone Density Index, pneumatic/endoskeletal architecture.
* **Layer III: Biochemical Elemental Core** — Internal bio-reactor elemental organs (pyro-sacs, electrocyte batteries, cryo-proteins, acid lymph glands).
* **Layer IV: Tectonic Speciation & Historical Epoch** — Continental plate drift phenomena explaining evolutionary adaptations.

### 3. Interactive Continental Drift Tectonic Map
An interactive vector cartography view demonstrating the breakup of the supercontinent into modern regional plates (Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, Paldea) and the temporal vortex of Area Zero.

### 4. Technical Stack
* React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
* Tailwind CSS
* Web Audio API (100% synthetic, zero audio files loaded)
* Trilingual Localization: English, Indonesian, Japanese
